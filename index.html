Meet the Artist

Naika Ishi Gumila

BS Information Technology · 3rd Year

I am a student with a passion for technology, creativity, and innovation. I enjoy developing digital solutions that address real-world problems, particularly in healthcare, education, and automation. Through my projects, I aim to create practical, accessible, and user-friendly solutions that can make people's lives easier. I am continuously learning and improving my skills in design, programming, and problem-solving while exploring new ideas that allow me to grow both personally and academically.

Artistic Interests

My artistic interests include digital art, graphic design, photography, and exploring creative ways to express ideas through visuals. I enjoy combining technology and creativity to create meaningful and visually appealing designs. I am also interested in experimenting with different art forms to discover new ways of expressing myself and appreciating the beauty of art.

My Approach to Art

My personal approach to art is to see it as a way of expressing myself, exploring creativity, and communicating ideas beyond words. I believe that art does not have to be perfect to be meaningful because every artwork reflects the artist's thoughts, emotions, and experiences. I enjoy exploring different styles and forms of art, especially those that combine creativity and technology. Through art, I hope to discover new perspectives, appreciate beauty in everyday life, and express my individuality.

“Art allows me to see the world beyond what I already know.”

Blackout Poetry· Individual

Babae

Blackout poetry is created by selecting meaningful words from an existing text and covering the remaining words with dark marks or illustrations. The chosen words form a new poem with its own message.

Lesson learned: I learned that words can have new meanings depending on how they are selected and arranged. This activity encouraged me to express my thoughts creatively and discover hidden messages in ordinary texts.

Lesson learned: I learned that art reflects the culture, beliefs, and experiences of the period in which it was created. Studying art through time helped me appreciate different styles and understand how artistic expression continues to evolve.

Moments Through My Lens

Photography: Seeing the Extraordinary in the Ordinary.

Photo 01

A Moment of Tenderness

Location / subject: Indoor setting — a baby resting comfortably on a bed while being gently comforted by an adult.

This photograph captures a peaceful moment between a baby and a caregiver. The baby's relaxed expression and the gentle touch of the adult create a feeling of warmth, love, and security.

Concept / technique: Portrait photography, soft natural-looking lighting, shallow depth of field, warm color tones, and candid photography.

[A little moment of love that speaks louder than words.

Photo 02

A Good Morning

Location / subject: Indoor setting — a sleeping baby lying on a soft bed with one hand resting near the face.

This photograph highlights the innocence and peacefulness of infancy. The baby's sleeping expression and curled-up position create an intimate and heartwarming image that celebrates the beauty of life's simplest moments.

Concept / technique: Close-up photography, soft lighting, shallow depth of field, warm color grading, and emphasis on facial details and natural expressions.

In the quietest moments, the sweetest memories are made.

PUBMAT

Visual Communication

Birthday Poster

A Celebration of 21st

Purpose / audience: To promote and invite friends, family, and loved ones to a 21st birthday celebration by presenting the event details in an attractive and memorable design.

Design concept: The design features a large metallic number 21, blue balloons, and a light background to create a festive and elegant atmosphere. The use of photo cutouts adds a personal touch, while the blue and silver color palette gives the poster a modern and celebratory look.

Tools / techniques: Canva: Digital photo editing, background removal, image compositing, typography, color coordination, layering, and decorative element placement.

Music Poster

After Hours

Purpose / audience: To promote a music and entertainment event to young audiences, music enthusiasts, and people interested in live performances and social activities.

Design concept: The design uses a dark background with pink, blue, and purple lighting to create an energetic nightlife atmosphere. The featured drummer emphasizes the live music experience, while bold typography and contrasting colors make the event information visually striking and easy to notice.

Tools / techniques: Canva: Digital photo editing, typography, color grading, contrast adjustment, image cropping, lighting effects, texture overlays, and visual hierarchy.

Final Reflection

Looking Forward: Who Am I Becoming?

My journey in Art Appreciation has helped me understand that art is more than just creating beautiful things. It is a way of expressing emotions, sharing ideas, and seeing the world from different perspectives. Throughout this course, I discovered that art can be found in many forms, from visual artworks and photography to graphic design and music. Each activity gave me an opportunity to explore my creativity and appreciate the meaning behind different forms of artistic expression. One of the most meaningful parts of this journey was having the opportunity to try different artistic activities. Some experiences challenged me to think creatively and explore ideas beyond what I was used to. There were times when expressing my ideas was difficult, but these challenges taught me to be more patient, open-minded, and willing to learn. I realized that creating art does not always mean achieving perfection; sometimes, the most important part is the process of discovering what I can do and what I can improve. Through these experiences, I also learned to appreciate not only my own work but also the creativity and perspectives of others. Art has taught me that everyone has a unique way of expressing themselves, and every artwork can tell a story or communicate a message. This realization encouraged me to look beyond the surface and understand the thought, emotion, and purpose behind each creation. Looking forward, I see myself becoming a more creative, confident, and open-minded individual. I want to continue exploring different forms of art, improving my skills, and finding new ways to express my thoughts and ideas. I know that I still have much to learn, but this journey has reminded me that growth happens when I am willing to experiment, make mistakes, and step outside my comfort zone. Ultimately, Art Appreciation has helped me realize that art is not only about what I create but also about how I see and understand the world around me. As I continue my journey, I hope to carry the lessons I have learned into my future experiences and use creativity as a way to express myself, connect with others, and discover more about who I am becoming.

- Naika Ishi Gumila

“Art is not only something I create. It is also a way of seeing, questioning, and understanding the world.”
